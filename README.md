Two Lines to Your Past Self: a program that writes exactly two lines — line one warns the writer's past self about one specific moment in a single sentence, and line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. A judge model compares entries two at a time, weighing how concrete the moment is and how clearly the second line traces cause and effect.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (262 characters, two lines, one sentence each).
